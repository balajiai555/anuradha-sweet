Our Heritage Story

18+ Years of Spreading Sweetness in Kaptanganj

Founded on 30th July, 2006, Anuradha Sweet and Restaurant has been a beloved culinary landmark in Kaptanganj, Kushinagar.

What started as a heartfelt mission to serve pure, adulteration-free traditional sweets made with 100% pure desi ghee has blossomed into the town's premier sweet shop and multi-cuisine vegetarian restaurant.

Every festival—from Diwali and Raksha Bandhan to weddings and auspicious celebrations—families across Kushinagar trust Anuradha Sweet for unmatched purity, handcrafted elegance, and authentic taste passed down through generations.
